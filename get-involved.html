Where it goes

Where the support goes

No overhead, no salaries — we are students. Sponsorship pays for the things enthusiasm cannot cover.

  • Contest registrations. Entry fees for Kangaroo, CTMC, and the Waterloo contests, so cost is never the reason a student sits one out.
  • Camp materials. Problem sets, printing, prizes, and the puzzles that make a summer camp feel like a summer camp.
  • Livestream and webinar tools. The software and equipment behind our YouTube sessions and guest speaker nights.
  • Charity events. Seed costs for the math-themed fundraisers that sent $10,320 to SickKids — every dollar raised goes straight through.

Become a sponsor

If your organization wants more students solving hard problems together, we would love to talk. Sponsorship can be funding, materials, venue space, or an hour of a working mathematician's time.
